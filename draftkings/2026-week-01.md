# Week 1 Card, v2 (Sun Sep 13 / Mon Sep 14) + Week 2 TNF lean (Thu Sep 17)

Written Saturday Sep 12, 2026. Version 2 replaces the consensus-driven v1 with a card built from
the lines themselves: implied probabilities, key numbers, where DraftKings sits versus the market,
correlation between legs, and the size of DK's hold by market type.
Confirm every number in the DK app before placing. Lines move.

## Method (why this card looks different from what is online)

1. Price the legs, not the narratives. A leg goes on the card only if my estimated win rate beats the
   breakeven implied by the price. Breakeven at -110 is 52.4 percent.
2. Shop the number against the market. When DK is a point off consensus and through a key number
   (3, 7, 10), that is the whole edge. This week that is Chargers -10.5 at DK while the market sits at -9 to -10.
3. Respect Week 1 uncertainty. Lines are priced on last year's film. Underdogs of 6.5 or more are 32-13-1
   ATS in Week 1 since 2013 (71 percent). Favorites of 7 or more win 75 to 80 percent outright but cover
   only about 48 percent long term. So: big favorites on the moneyline only if the price is fair, big dogs on the spread.
4. Put parlay money where DK's hold is smallest. Spreads and totals carry about 4.5 percent hold. Player
   props carry 8 to 12 percent. Same game parlays carry 15 to 30 percent because DK reprices correlation.
   Cross-game parlays of individually good legs are the best vehicle. One SGP stays on the card at a quarter unit.
5. Correlate on purpose. Underdog plus under is positively correlated (dogs win low-scoring games), so a
   two-leg dog-and-under ticket wins more often than the product of its legs.
6. Fade the crowd where the crowd is loudest. 93 percent of tickets on Bears -3 as a road favorite is the
   worst spot on the board, not the best.

## DraftKings build notes (only DraftKings)

1. Core singles: Spread or Total from the game card. Standard -110. Take DK's number, not the number on this card, if they differ, and apply the kill switch.
2. P1, P2, P3: cross-game parlays. Add one leg from each game and DK builds a standard Parlay. Do not toggle Same Game Parlay. DK's displayed odds should be within 10 percent of the estimate on this card. If they are lower than that, one leg's price has moved. Check it.
3. P4: both legs are from one game, so DK will automatically convert it to a Same Game Parlay and reprice the correlation. Accept at +200 or better. Below +200, split it into two straight bets at 0.25u each (Broncos +2.5 and Under 43.5) and skip the parlay.
4. P5: Same Game Parlay by design. Accept at +350 or better. Below +350, skip.
5. Promos: check the Promos tab before placing. A No Sweat SGP token goes on P5 (the ticket below breakeven). A Profit Boost token goes on P3 (the highest-edge ticket). Odds boosts on individual games are only worth it if they land on a leg already on this card.
6. Cash Out: do not use it. DK prices cash out against you. The one exception is a parlay sitting on its final leg where the kill switch for that leg has triggered (a late scratch).
7. Moneyline prices on this card (Texans +108, Vikings -122, Broncos +124) are Saturday numbers. If DK shows Texans below +100 or Broncos below +110 at bet time, drop that leg and run P3 as a two-leg.

## Reality check
Nothing here is guaranteed. My win estimates are judgments, not measurements, and a 58 percent leg still
loses 42 times out of 100. The reason to prefer this card over the consensus card is not that it wins this
week. It is that every leg has a stated edge against its price, the money sits in the markets where DK's
hold is lowest, and the stakes are sized so that a 0-for-8 Sunday costs 4.75 units and not the bankroll.
