## Reality check
No one can guarantee winning parlays. Every leg here is priced near 50 to 60 percent, and a three-leg parlay of those lands about one in six times at roughly 6 to 1. The edge, if there is one, comes from injury and line-movement information the market has not fully priced, and from disciplined sizing. That is why the total risk is capped at 5u and long shots are quarter units. Expect losing weeks. Judge the process over the season, not the Sunday.
